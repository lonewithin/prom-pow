Live · Fitness PWA

Promise Workout

A beginner-friendly fat-loss coach that runs in your pocket.

HTML/JSGeolocationSpeechWake LockPWA
The problem

Most workout apps are paywalled, bloated or built for people who are already fit. Walking distance was another app's job.

What I built

Guided full-body sessions with a leg day every week, built around 2×10kg dumbbells, a push-up board, an ab roller and a sit-up bar. Each exercise has an animated demo and a written tutorial. A voice coach and drift-free timers run the session. A GPS walk tracker with Kalman smoothing and jump rejection tracks distance. It installs on the iPhone home screen and works offline.

SOLKIT

Live · X tool

Unfollow Helper

See who doesn't follow back, with no bots and no login.

JavaScriptFileReaderlocalStorage
The problem

Finding who doesn't follow you back usually means risky bots or apps that want your X login.

What I built

A tool that runs entirely on your device. Import your official X data archive (following.js / follower.js) or paste handles, and it lists who isn't following back, plus fans you don't follow. Open each profile and unfollow yourself, then tick Done. It makes no network calls and never automates anything.

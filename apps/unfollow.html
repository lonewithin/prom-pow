Unfollow Helper

Find out who doesn't follow you back. Then unfollow them yourself, one tap at a time. Nothing is sent anywhere.

1 · Following

Upload following.js, or paste handles or links.

2 · Followers

Upload follower.js, or paste handles or links.

    Built by PROM · @promiseishere. This tool makes no network calls.

    How to use

    1. On X go to Settings › Your account › Download an archive of your data. When it's ready (it can take a day or more), unzip it.
    2. Upload data/following.js in box 1 and data/follower.js in box 2. You can also paste @handles or x.com links, one per line.
    3. Tap Compare lists. "Not following back" is everyone you follow who doesn't follow you. "Fans" are people who follow you that you don't follow.
    4. Tap Open profile, unfollow on X yourself, then tick Done.
    5. Go slow. Unfollowing lots of accounts quickly can hit X's limits or flag your account. Do a few dozen, then take a break.

    Archive files only have account IDs, so those profiles open by ID. Your files never leave this device. Done marks are kept in this browser until you clear them.

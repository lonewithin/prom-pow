Hidden note

Limits, read first
  • Text up to 500 characters. It is encrypted with your password, then hidden in the lowest bit of each pixel.
  • The saved image is a PNG. Send it as a file. X, WhatsApp, Telegram photos and screenshots recompress images and destroy the hidden text.
  • Large photos are scaled to 1600 px on the long side. A full 500 characters needs an image of about 80 × 80 px or larger; the page tells you if it is too small.
  • A lost password cannot be recovered.

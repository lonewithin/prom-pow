How it works

Put a post’s text on the lines under its link and it attaches to that card. An ID or wallet goes in only if the post asks for one. If nothing is asked, you just get the heartfelt lines.

Reply on X opens x.com’s reply box for that exact post with the draft filled in. On a phone with X installed this usually jumps straight into the app.

Open in X app forces the app (iOS: twitter:// link, Android: an intent for the X app that falls back to the browser). Both buttons also copy the draft, so if the app opens without the text, just long-press and paste.

Nothing posts by itself. You tap Post in X every time.

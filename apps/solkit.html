Address rules

A Solana address is base58. The alphabet is 1-9 and A-Z, a-z, except 0, O, I, and l. Those four were left out because they look like each other.

A prefix or suffix cannot contain 0, O, I, or l. Ignore case only matches A and a. It does not create the missing characters.

The first character is not spread evenly. Most addresses start with 1-9 or A-H. A prefix that starts with J or later, or with any small letter, is about 17 to 60 times rarer.

One or two characters is quick. Three is a short wait. Four exact characters is still about a minute on a computer when the first one is 1-9 or A-H, and much longer otherwise. A phone is slower. With ignore case, four letters is several times faster. The line above shows the real odds before you wait.
